# Lin Chong — identity direction

Use the approved Classic sprites for rendering, pixel-cluster treatment and head/body scale. Do not use Song Jiang's facial features, cloth cap, pointed goatee, paired decorative robe bands, or red/gold costume as Lin Chong's design.

Project description (`js/data/chars.js`): Panther Head; former Imperial Guard arms instructor; medium athletic build; long face, round eyes, thick brows, black topknot and short beard. His battle weapon is the Serpent Spear.

Current portrait sources:

- `assets/portraits/linchong-official-expressions.png`: green formal instructor outfit, subtle brocade, neat layered collar, green hair bindings and small silver hair ornaments.
- `assets/portraits/linchong-exile-expressions.png`: weathered slate-blue travelling outfit, rough grey-brown mantle, high messy topknot, loose hair, blue headband and a vertical exile mark on his right cheek. Short beard follows the jaw; it is not Song Jiang's long pointed chin beard.

The game switches portraits at `ch3_exiled` and defaults to exile after Chapter 3. The user chose **both outfits, matching story progression**. Distinguish costume selection from approval of a particular generated design.

Walking design: unarmed, readable eye whites, consistent details across directions, no rectangular head/shoulder lock. Preserve the cheek mark on the correct anatomical side; a marked profile cannot simply be mirrored for the other direction.

Battle design: exactly one static, dynamic left-facing pose using the Serpent Spear. Keep the body scale and extend the canvas for the spear.

Prepare detailed 192×256 frames directly from original sources with original colours, and preview beside Song Jiang on actual game backgrounds. Keep sources and prompts. Do not install a new Lin Chong design into the production manifest before its review.
